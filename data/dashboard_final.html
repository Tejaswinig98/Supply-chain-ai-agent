How the numbers are computed

No black box — the same four formulas run every recommendation on this page.

Reorder point

When on-hand stock should trigger a new order, accounting for lead time.

reorder_pt = avg_daily_demand × lead_time + safety_stock

Safety stock

Buffer against demand variability during the vulnerable lead-time window, sized to a target service level.

safety_stock = z(service_level) × σ × √lead_time

Economic order quantity

The order size that minimizes the combined cost of ordering and holding inventory.

EOQ = √( 2 × annual_demand × order_cost ÷ holding_cost )

Route optimization

Great-circle distance between warehouses, sequenced by nearest-neighbor then refined with 2-opt swaps.

minimize Σ distance(stop[i], stop[i+1])
